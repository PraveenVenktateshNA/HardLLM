The size of these cells through complex remodeling is more than 10-fold and has a number of effects including chronic imoemia, skin cancer, genetic changes, altered immune systems, aging, genetic changes, demyelinating tumors such as cancer or hereditary tumors, and relapsing to other tissues. Trouble developing skin lesions of different sizes increases the difficulty because these lesions form in different cell types.
